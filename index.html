The Art of Home Improvements That Add Value

What to Upgrade, What to Avoid, and How to Get the Best Return on Your Investment

By Richard C. Brown

Make every home improvement decision count. This practical guide helps homeowners think through what to upgrade, what to avoid, and how to spend more wisely—from kitchens and bathrooms to outdoor spaces, energy improvements, and curb appeal.

Whether you’re preparing to sell or making your home work better for you, learn to set priorities, compare detailed written bids, and feel more confident about the choices ahead.
